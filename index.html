01 / THE CHALLENGE

Talk to the
Earth.

Spoken audio is fast, expressive and natural. In time-critical remote-sensing scenarios, it lets users describe geographic targets without typing detailed queries.

RemoteAIUC is the first benchmark dedicated to audio-guided remote-sensing understanding. We invite the multimedia community to explore direct alignment between speech and Earth-observation imagery, preserving the emphasis and prosody that text leaves behind.

Organized for ICASSP 2027 · Remote sensing · Speech · Multimodal learning

02 / TWO WAYS IN

Choose your
frontier.

Two complementary tasks. One shared goal: robust audio–visual grounding for Earth observation.

TRACK 01

Remote-ARS

Audio Referring
Segmentation

Given a remote-sensing image and an audio referring expression, predict a pixel-level mask for the object or region being described.

Pixel-levelAudio grounding
TRACK 02

Remote-AIR

Audio–Image
Retrieval

Given an audio query or an RS image, retrieve its most semantically relevant counterpart from the opposite modality.

BidirectionalCross-modal
