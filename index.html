✦Understanding

Spoken audio is a fast, expressive and natural interface for geospatial artificial intelligence. In time-critical remote-sensing scenarios, people can describe a target without typing a detailed query.

RSAIU establishes a transparent benchmark for direct alignment between audio expressions and remote-sensing imagery. Unlike an ASR-to-text cascade, our tasks preserve emphasis, rhythm and speaking style.

Who should participate?Remote sensing · Speech and audio · Computer vision · Multimodal learning · Geospatial AI

03 / CHALLENGE

Two tracks.
One frontier.

Explore fine-grained grounding and bidirectional retrieval between the spoken word and the overhead image.

TRACK 01ARS
◌

Audio Referring
Segmentation

Given an RS image and an audio referring expression, predict a pixel-level mask for the referred object or region.

  • Multi-scale feature fusion
  • Boundary-aware mask decoding
  • One-to-zero, one-to-one and one-to-many targets
Dataset details ↗
TRACK 02AIR
⇄

Audio–Image
Retrieval

Given an audio query or an RS image, retrieve its most semantically relevant counterpart from the opposite modality.

  • Audio-to-image and image-to-audio
  • Joint audio–image embeddings
  • Contrastive learning and hard negatives
